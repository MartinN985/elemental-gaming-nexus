Briefing

The Apocalypse will be podcasted.

The Apocalypse happened. Unfortunately, these idiots somehow survived it.

Our Crawlers are thrown into the dungeon, where survival is entertainment, every floor is trying to kill them, millions of viewers are watching, and the System AI may be the most dangerous thing of all.

Expect roleplay, overthought simple problems, questionable tactical decisions, and everything becoming the GM’s problem.
