Briefing

It ain’t so pure. It ain’t much of a bay.

In 1964, five towns became one county.

About ten thousand people, dirt roads, one highway, a Walmart that ate the shops on Main Street, and a toy factory dumping pollution into the air. Surrounding it all is The Bed: a dead lake full of stranded ships, land races, and things people do where the lights do not reach.

Deputy Jaime Prospero came down from Chicago trying to make detective. Deputy Tim Brown has lived here his whole life and wants to keep the town safe. Florian Carter grew up getting shoved around by the same families, made it out, and has returned to do some method acting.

A body on Buckberry Farm starts an investigation that will take them into the darkness of Purity Bay.
