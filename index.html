About

Who We Are

We’re a group of PhD and master’s students, doctors, game designers, and computer scientists who enjoy playing tabletop games together.

That’s Redacted and Purity Bay are our Delta Green campaigns. Dungeon Crawler Degenerates is our Dungeon Crawler Carl game. We’re nerdy, we’re ourselves, and we have a good time at the table.
